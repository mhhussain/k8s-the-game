Three ways to go light & playful

The dark control-room look is out. Here are three distinct light directions, each shown as the real UI — a node card, a ticket, status pills and a button — so you can read them at a glance. Pick one and I'll roll it across every screen. All three keep the hard rule: green = healthy, amber = degrading, red = failing, instantly legible on a bright surface.

Status colors stay legible everywhere

Across all three directions the health language is consistent and reads instantly on a light background — tinted pill fills with saturated text and a glowing LED, never a wall of color. The built game also ships all three as a live theme switch in Tweaks, so you can A/B them on real screens before committing.
