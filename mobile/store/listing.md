## Description

Welcome to prod-east-1. You have one small node, an empty registry, and stakeholders who describe performance problems as “vibes.”

Kubernetes: The Game is a menu-driven infrastructure management sim. Read tickets, build container images, deploy services, and keep your cluster healthy while traffic spikes and nodes go offline. No terminal required.

GROW YOUR LITTLE PRODUCTION EMPIRE
Manage five application repositories. Balance replicas, hardware, running costs, and traffic income. Every ticket is checked against your actual cluster, and completed requests remain promises you need to keep.

AUTOMATE THE AFTERNOON
Unlock twelve upgrades, from Deployments and health probes to continuous integration, rolling updates, autoscaling, and self-healing. Reach Secrets & Data Tier to finish the campaign, then keep playing in endless mode.

LEARN AS YOU GO
A skippable first-shift guide and offline glossary introduce the terminology. Experienced operators can jump straight in. This is a playful simulation, not a production configuration tool.

PLAY ON YOUR TERMS
Pause anytime or choose 1×, 2×, or 4× speed. Your cluster waits when you leave the app: no offline penalties, timers demanding your return, or push notifications. Enjoy three visual themes and layouts for phones and tablets.

ONE PURCHASE. THE WHOLE GAME.
Entirely offline. No accounts, ads, subscriptions, in-app purchases, leaderboards, or analytics. Local autosave and three manual slots keep your progress handy. Export and import JSON files to transfer saves manually between devices and the web game.

## Review notes

No login, account, network connection, demo credentials, subscription, or additional purchase is required. Tap Start your shift, then Resume. The first ticket arrives after three ticks (about seven seconds). The optional tutorial explains image building, service deployment, and ticket completion. Pause and resume are always available in gameplay. Saves & transfers uses the system file picker/share sheet only when requested by the player.

The app simulates infrastructure; it does not connect to or control real Kubernetes clusters. No developer telemetry is included. Privacy policy is readable offline under More → Privacy & support.
